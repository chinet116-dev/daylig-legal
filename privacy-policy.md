## What We Collect

**Account Information**
When you register, we collect your email 
address to create and manage your account.

**Habit & Check-in Data**
We collect the habits you create and your 
daily check-in records to provide the core 
app functionality.

**Usage Data**
We collect anonymized data about how you 
use AI coaching features to manage service 
costs and improve the product.

## How We Use Your Data

- To provide and maintain the Daylig service
- To sync your data across devices
- To generate personalized AI coaching reports
- To send push notifications you configure
- To respond to your support requests

## AI Features

Daylig uses Anthropic's Claude AI to generate 
weekly reports and coaching responses. Your 
habit data is sent to Anthropic's API to 
generate these responses. AI suggestions are 
not medical advice.

## Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase 
(hosted on AWS). Data is encrypted in transit 
and at rest.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.
We share data only with:
- Supabase (database hosting)
- Anthropic (AI processing)
- RevenueCat (subscription management)
- Apple / Google (payment processing)

## Your Rights

You can:
- Export all your data as CSV from Settings
- Delete your account and all data from Settings
- Contact wjung521@gmail.com for data requests

## Children's Privacy

Daylig is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We will notify you of significant changes 
via email or in-app notice.
